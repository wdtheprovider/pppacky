This privacy policy includes all the details about the data collected in KairZa and how it's used.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. The Personal Information that we collect is used for providing and improving the Service. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. KairZa has been built with the support and trust of its users, and we are committed to maintaining that trust.

### Information Collected from Users

- Phone Number (for authentication)
- Reminder Data (titles, descriptions, due dates, times)
- Tags and Priority Levels
- Color Preferences
- Recurrence Settings
- Completion Status
- Location Data (when location-based reminders are enabled - Coming Soon)
- Device Information (type, model, OS version)

The above information is required in order for a user to create and manage reminders within the app. Phone number authentication is handled securely through Firebase Auth. Location access (when available) will be used solely to trigger location-based reminders. All reminder data is stored locally on your device and optionally synced to the cloud with KairZa Premium.

### Data Storage and Security

**Local Storage:**
All reminder data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database. The app works fully offline without requiring internet connectivity. Local data is encrypted using platform-specific security measures.

**Cloud Storage (Premium Feature):**
When you subscribe to KairZa Premium, your data is synchronized to Firebase Firestore. Data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL protocols, and data at rest is encrypted using Firebase's security infrastructure.

### Location Information

KairZa may request access to your device's location in order to provide location-based reminder triggers (Coming Soon). Location data is used only when you explicitly enable this feature for specific reminders and is not continuously tracked.

### Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to your phone number, device information, and reminder data. The information that we request will be retained by us and used as described in this privacy policy.

### How to Request Account Deletion

If you decide that you no longer wish to use the KairZa service, you can request deletion of your account directly within the app. To do so, navigate to the **Settings** screen and select the account deletion option. Once confirmed, your account and associated personal information will be permanently removed from our cloud servers within 30 days in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Local data will be removed immediately from your device.

The app does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

### Premium Subscription

KairZa uses RevenueCat to manage premium subscriptions. Payment processing is handled by the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). We do not store your payment information. RevenueCat may collect subscription status and transaction data to provide the service.

### Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your reminder data is private and only accessible to you. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** Firebase and RevenueCat to provide core functionality
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect our rights
- **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger or acquisition (with prior notice)

### Cookies

This Service does not use cookies explicitly. However, third-party services such as Firebase may use cookies to collect information and improve their services.

### Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Service, provide the Service on our behalf, perform Service-related services, or assist us in analyzing how our Service is used.

These third parties include:
- **Firebase:** For authentication, cloud storage, and analytics
- **RevenueCat:** For subscription management
- **WorkManager:** For background reminder processing

### Security

We value your trust in providing us with your Personal Information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. We implement:

- End-to-end encryption for data in transit
- Encrypted local storage
- Secure authentication tokens
- Regular security audits

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### Data Retention

- Local data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data
- Cloud data is retained as long as your account is active
- Upon account deletion, all cloud data is permanently removed within 30 days
- Backup data is retained for 90 days for recovery purposes

### Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access:** View all your reminder data within the app
- **Modify:** Edit or update your reminders at any time
- **Delete:** Remove individual reminders or all data
- **Export:** Download your data in JSON format (Premium feature)
- **Opt-Out:** Disable cloud sync and use the app offline-only

### Children's Privacy

KairZa is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will delete such information.

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that are different from the laws of your country. We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws through Firebase and RevenueCat's compliance certifications.
