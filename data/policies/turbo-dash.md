This privacy policy includes all the details about the data collected in Turbo Dash and how it's used.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. The Personal Information that we collect is used for providing and improving the Service. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Turbo Dash has been built with the support and trust of its players, and we are committed to maintaining that trust.

### Information Collected from Users

- Game Progress Data (high scores, ghost run replays, coins balance)
- Owned Content (unlocked cars, purchased upgrades, Pit Crew Pass status)
- In-App Purchase History (transaction records managed by the App Store or Google Play)
- Device Information (type, model, OS version)

No account registration or personal identification (such as email or phone number) is required to play Turbo Dash. The above information is stored locally on your device and is used solely to provide a persistent game experience across sessions.

### Data Storage and Security

**Local Storage:**  
All game progress, high scores, owned items, and preferences are stored locally on your device using platform-provided key-value storage (`SharedPreferences`). The app functions fully offline without requiring internet connectivity. Local data is protected by platform-specific security measures provided by iOS and Android.

**No Cloud Storage:**  
Turbo Dash does not currently operate its own cloud backend. Your game data is never uploaded to our servers.

### In-App Purchases

Turbo Dash offers optional in-app purchases:

- **Turbo Coins** (consumable) — virtual currency used to unlock cars and upgrades within the game (`starter`, `turbo`, `mega` packs)
- **Pit Crew Pass** (non-consumable) — a permanent upgrade granting extra crash lives and instant repairs (`get_the_pass`)

All payment processing is handled entirely by the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information, credit card details, or billing address. Purchase records are managed by Apple and Google in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

### Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, certain game state information (scores, owned items, equipped loadout) is retained locally on your device as described in this policy. This information is used exclusively to restore your progress when you relaunch the app and is never transmitted off your device by Turbo Dash itself.

### How to Delete Your Data

Since Turbo Dash does not operate a cloud account system, all your data resides on your device. You can remove all data at any time by:

1. Uninstalling the app — this removes all locally stored game progress.
2. Clearing the app's data via your device's Settings → Apps → Turbo Dash → Clear Data (Android).
3. Offloading or deleting the app on iOS (Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Turbo Dash).

For purchase restoration after reinstalling, use the **Restore Purchases** option in the Pit Stop Shop, which queries the App Store or Google Play directly.

The app does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Link to the privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the app:

- [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Apple App Store](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

### Premium Content and Purchases

Turbo Dash uses the platform's native in-app purchase system to manage consumable coin packs and the non-consumable Pit Crew Pass. Payment processing and transaction records are handled by the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). We do not store your payment information. Purchase verification is performed directly with the platform store.

### Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your game data is private and only accessible to you on your device. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect our rights
- **Platform Stores:** Apple and Google receive transaction data when you make a purchase, governed by their own privacy policies

### Cookies

Turbo Dash does not use cookies. The app does not use a web browser interface or web tracking technologies.

### Service Providers

The following third-party services are integrated into Turbo Dash:

- **Apple App Store / Google Play Store:** For processing in-app purchases and managing purchase receipts
- **Flame Game Engine:** An open-source Flutter game engine used for rendering — it does not collect any user data

### Security

We value your trust in providing us with your game data, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. We implement:

- Local data storage protected by iOS and Android platform security sandboxing
- No transmission of personal data over the network by Turbo Dash
- Purchase validation performed entirely through official platform APIs

However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### Data Retention

- Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
- No cloud data is retained by Turbo Dash
- Purchase history is retained by Apple or Google in accordance with their respective policies

### Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access:** View all your game progress and owned items within the app
- **Modify:** Progress is updated in real time as you play
- **Delete:** Remove all local data by uninstalling or clearing app data
- **Restore Purchases:** Re-grant previously purchased non-consumable items via the in-app restore function

### Children's Privacy

Turbo Dash is a family-friendly racing game. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Because no account creation or personal data submission is required to play, the risk of inadvertent collection is minimal. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns, please contact us and we will address them promptly.

### International Data Transfers

Turbo Dash does not transfer your personal data internationally. All game data is stored locally on your device. Any data handled by Apple or Google in connection with purchases is subject to their own privacy policies and international transfer compliance frameworks.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.

Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
