This privacy policy includes all the details about the data collected in UniAPS and how it's used.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. The Personal Information that we collect is used for providing and improving the Service. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. As a user, we also do not like to give personal information and we don't want to see our information collected without knowing what will be going to happen to those data. UniAPS App has been alive with the support and trust of its users. We want to keep the trust; you can find all the details about the data usage below.

### Information Collected from Users

To create an account and access the app's main features, the following information is required:

- **Name**
- **Email Address**
- **Profile Picture**
- **Username** (derived from your display name)

This data is safely stored in a secured Firebase Firestore database. Users can sign in using **Google Sign-In** or **Apple Sign-In** (iOS only). These methods are secure, fast, and delegate authentication to the respective identity provider.

When you sign in, a **referral code** is automatically generated and stored on your account for the referral program.

### Results

Results are only stored on your device locally. You can clear this data on the marks management screen.

### Clicks

Clicks are a core part of the app's functionality. They are stored locally on your device. We do not store your remaining click count in the cloud. Make sure to use your clicks before uninstalling the app or clearing the app's saved data, as they cannot be recovered.

To recharge clicks, you can purchase them through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Purchase transaction records (including product ID, platform, and associated user ID) are stored in our Firebase Firestore database for verification and support purposes.

### Points and Daily Check-In

UniAPS includes a Karma Points system. Points are earned through daily check-ins and other in-app activities. Your points balance and last check-in date are stored in our Firebase Firestore database and are associated with your account.

### Rooms (Community Feature)

UniAPS provides a Rooms feature that allows users to create and join community rooms. When you use this feature:

- Your **user ID** and **room membership role** are stored in Firestore as part of the room's member list.
- Your **push notification subscription ID** (OneSignal) is stored in the room membership record to enable room-specific push notifications.
- Messages and activity within public rooms may be visible to other members of that room.

### Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to a username and academic results. The information that we request will be retained by us and used as described in this privacy policy.

### Push Notifications

UniAPS uses **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** and **OneSignal** to deliver push notifications. When you grant notification permissions:

- A device-specific FCM token is generated and stored in your user record in Firestore.
- Your OneSignal subscription ID is stored to enable targeted notifications for Rooms activity and app updates.
- You can withdraw notification permission at any time through your device settings. Your device token will be removed from our servers when you log out.

### App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS devices, UniAPS may request permission to track your activity across apps and websites using Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. This is used to deliver relevant advertisements. You may accept or decline this request at any time from your device's Privacy settings.

### Advertising

UniAPS App may display advertisements in various places within the application. The advertising system may use and transmit basic regional and language information about you to provide relevant ads. In some cases, advertisements may be hidden for users with active sponsorships or premium subscriptions. Even in those cases, basic anonymized data may still be shared with advertising partners.

### How to Request Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the **Profile** screen within the app. Tap the "Delete Account" option and confirm your decision. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Please note that if you sign in using **Google** or **Apple**, no password is required to delete your account — you can proceed directly from the Profile screen.

Upon account deletion, your user data will be removed from our servers. Purchase records may be retained for legal and financial compliance purposes.
