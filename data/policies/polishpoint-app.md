If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. The Personal Information that we collect is used for providing and improving the Service. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Polish Point has been built with the support and trust of its users, and we are committed to maintaining that trust.

### Information Collected from users

- Name
- Surname
- Phone Number
- Email Address
- Location Data
- Pictures and Images

The above information is required in order for a user to create and manage an account within the app. Location access is used solely to help users discover nearby salons. Pictures are used for profile information and salon-related content such as listings and galleries.

### Location Information

Polish Point may request access to your device’s location in order to display salons near your current location. Location data is used only while the app is in use and is not continuously tracked.

### Pictures and Media

The app may request access to your device’s camera or photo library to allow you to upload profile pictures or salon-related images. These images are only visible within the app and are not shared with third parties unless required to provide the service.

### Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to your name, surname, phone number, email address, approximate location, and images. The information that we request will be retained by us and used as described in this privacy policy.

### How to request account deletion

If you decide that you no longer wish to use the Polish Point service, you can request deletion of your account directly within the app. To do so, navigate to the **Profile** tab and scroll down to find the account deletion option. Once confirmed, your account and associated personal information will be permanently removed in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
